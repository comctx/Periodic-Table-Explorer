### Required review for each entry

1. Open **original** and **source** URLs; confirm the *same* image, author, and subject (actual sample / compound / apparatus / simulation / placeholder).
2. Inspect the source file's current copyright/license notice and version history. For CC photos, capture exact license version, creator, title and modifications, preserve required attribution and links, and check commercial distribution and share-alike obligations.
3. For public-domain or government images, independently substantiate the public-domain basis and any separate notice or branding requirement. Do **not** assume that an image is free to distribute merely because the metadata says "public domain."
4. If the rights are unclear or the image is misleading, remove it from the release assets or replace it with verified original/licensed artwork. Do not copy restricted RSC site images without permission.
5. Verify the final displayed caption, original link, license link, alt text, local file existence/content, offline operation, and the Store-bundle contents; then mark the row as checked.

**Examples requiring extra care:** #94 plutonium ring's [Wikimedia Commons page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Plutonium_ring.jpg) describes special Los Alamos attribution/notice requirements not captured by the existing vague "Attribution" metadata; #96 curium has a different source and no explicit license statement. #84–87 and #101–104 are described as illustrations or unrelated experiments; #105–118 are generic placeholders.
