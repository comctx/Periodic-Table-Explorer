**Branch:** `scientific-audit-2026-10` (published `main` intentionally unchanged)  
**Review date:** 2026-10-15 (US Central/UTC)  
**Scope:** 20 actual app photo records and available original Wikimedia Commons/publisher source and copyright notices. Earlier GitHub Actions visual/dHash and gallery recovery runs established likely local-photo identity; this follow-up improves creator/licensing and scientific captions. **Image-source matching and CC claims are not whole-package commercial redistribution clearance.**

## Results

All **20 image entries #21–40** have a documented original photo URL, a source-license statement and an element-specific subject description. This follow-up corrected the on-device scientific caption for iron (#26) and tightened author, alteration and ShareAlike text for the other identified photographs. **No physical JPEG needed replacement in this 20-element batch**: #29 copper was already replaced with a Michigan native copper museum photo in the preceding work, and the 12 photos formerly mismatched to their original source have been recovered or replaced separately. **No extra images were added to the iOS stage**: 92 displayed candidates / 26 withheld or hidden placeholders remain.

## Key corrections and cautions

- **#26 Iron:** The publisher explicitly says the photograph depicts fragments of an iron meteorite at **about 92% iron**, not elemental iron refined to purity. The image title, About & Credits metadata and element-page heading/description now distinguish the *iron-rich meteorite* from a pure iron specimen. See [publisher iron sample description](https://images-of-elements.com/iron.php).
- **#21, #25, #28, #29, #39:** Source files carry **CC BY-SA 3.0 or 4.0**. Author, original link, exact license version, re-encoding and image-adaptation/ShareAlike obligations are now spelled out in metadata. Original source examples: [Scandium](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Scandium,_Sc.jpg), [Manganese](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Manganese_element.jpg), [Nickel](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nickel_chunk.jpg), [Copper](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mus%C3%A9um_Toulouse_-_cuivre_natif.JPG), [Yttrium](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Piece_of_Yttrium.jpg).
- **#22 Titanium:** Current JPEG is a **titanium sponge cylinder** rather than the separate shiny van Arkel–de Boer-process crystal shown on the [publisher page](https://images-of-elements.com/titanium.php). The photo remains accurately labeled as sponge; the individual photographer's name is still unverified.
- **#27 Cobalt, #30 Zinc, #31 Gallium:** Correct exact originals are the matching publisher-gallery JPEGs after previous source recovery. Their individual photographers are still unnamed; metadata correctly credits the publisher rather than inventing an uploader/creator identity.
- **#33 Arsenic and #34 Selenium:** Commons identifies the publication identity **Hi-Res Images of Chemical Elements**, rather than a particular individual person. The app now credits the publisher without guessing a photographer. Arsenic is sealed grey metallic arsenic; selenium depicts the **black amorphous allotrope**, not grey crystalline selenium. See [arsenic source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Arsenic_(33_As).jpg) and [selenium source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Selenium.jpg).
- **#35 Bromine:** The picture is sealed, liquid 99.5%-pure bromine in glass/acrylic; it does not portray bromine as safe to handle openly. [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bromine-ampoule.jpg).
- **#36 Krypton:** The colored glow is generated by **electrical excitation**; it is not ordinary unexcited krypton gas. [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Krypton-glow.jpg).
- **#37 Rubidium:** Named as a sample from the Dennis S.K. collection with superoxide coloring inside a sealed ampoule. Some Commons language captions say liquid, but the app carefully avoids implying rubidium is normally liquid at room temperature. [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rb5.JPG).
- **#38 Strontium:** The pieces are stored **under paraffin oil**; photo labels distinguish protective oil from the element. [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Strontium-1.jpg).
- **#39 Yttrium:** The file's German description explicitly states **6.21 g and minimum 99% pure**, so the app describes purity as a lower bound, not an exact assay. [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Piece_of_Yttrium.jpg).

## Release blockers that remain

1. **Individual photographer/rights chain remain incomplete for seven entries** (#22, #26, #27, #30, #31, #33, #34). Some publisher general CC BY 3.0 claims may cover them, but a publisher notice alone does not establish authorship or exclusions for each saved adapted JPEG. Do not mark them individually certified for redistribution.
2. The final package must retain each displayed image's author, title/source link, license link, resizing/cropping notes and any CC BY-SA adaptation obligations. Confirm the actual compressed JPEG corresponds to the credited original for each compiled release.
3. The iOS packaging script explicitly excludes 26 other image files, but **Microsoft Store/other installers** require equivalent distribution-file filtering. A passing static test is not a completed signed IPA or Store approval.
4. Continue picture-specific rights audit for **41–60**, including publisher authorship/derivative-credit issues, without merging into `main` until the full release review is complete.

**Repository status:** The prior 14 source-recovery discrepancies have been investigated; no new misidentified source-photo URLs arose in this batch. Only metadata, scientific captions, the iron detail-page label, tests and this report are changed here.
